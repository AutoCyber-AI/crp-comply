# Enterprise templates

This directory contains starter templates for Enterprise engagements. They are
provided as **starting points** and must be reviewed and customised by qualified
legal counsel before execution.

| Template | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| [NDA.md](templates/NDA.md) | Mutual non-disclosure agreement for design-partner and vendor discussions. |
| [DPA.md](templates/DPA.md) | Article 28 GDPR data-processing addendum (controller ↔ processor). |
| [MSA.md](templates/MSA.md) | Master services agreement governing subscriptions and services. |
| [SOW.md](templates/SOW.md) | Statement of work to attach to an MSA for specific engagements. |

## Usage

1. Copy the relevant template into your contracting workflow.
2. Replace all bracketed placeholders with party names, addresses, dates, and jurisdiction-specific terms.
3. Have the document reviewed by your legal counsel and the customer's legal counsel.
4. Execute the final version through your normal signature process.
